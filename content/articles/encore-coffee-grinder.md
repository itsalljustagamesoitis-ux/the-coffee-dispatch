Finding the right entry point into burr grinding is harder than it looks , the Baratza Encore has been the default recommendation for years, but the lineup has expanded enough that "just get the Encore" no longer covers it. There are now three distinct Encore variants alongside meaningful alternatives, and the differences between them matter depending on whether you're brewing filter coffee, pulling espresso shots, or both. A quick look at the full range of [coffee grinders](/grinders/) before you buy is worth the time.

The brief version: if you're brewing drip or pour-over, the original Encore handles it cleanly. If espresso is involved, the answer changes. Here's what I found after using these machines and why the distinctions aren't marketing noise.

## What to Look For in a Coffee Grinder

### Burr Type and Grind Consistency

The single most important variable in a coffee grinder is whether it uses burrs or a blade. A blade grinder spins a metal propeller through the beans, producing a chaotic mix of powder and coarse fragments. Uneven particle size means uneven extraction , some grounds over-extract while others stay under, and the result tastes simultaneously bitter and flat. I used a blade grinder for two weeks before understanding why my espresso was undrinkable. There is no workaround for this.

Burr grinders crush beans between two abrasive surfaces set at a fixed distance. Every particle that passes through emerges at roughly the same size. That consistency is what allows you to dial in a brew recipe and have it repeat reliably. Even a modest burr grinder is a categorical improvement over any blade model , this is not a matter of degree.

### Grind Range and Espresso Capability

Not all burr grinders cover the full range from coarse French press to fine espresso. Many entry-level models are optimized for filter brewing , coarser, more forgiving territory , and their adjustment steps are too large to dial in espresso with precision. Grinding for espresso requires micro-adjustments: changes small enough that a single step meaningfully shifts extraction time and flavor.

The distinction between a standard Encore and an ESP-designated model is real. An ESP grinder is calibrated with espresso in its range and offers finer step increments in that zone. If your brewing is split between a pour-over and a machine, that matters. If you only pull espresso, you may want to look past entry-level options entirely.

### Retention and Workflow

Grinder retention , the coffee that stays inside the chute and burr chamber after grinding , is a practical issue that most reviews underweight. High-retention grinders mix old grounds with fresh ones on every dose, blunting flavor and making it difficult to taste a single-origin coffee at its best. For anyone buying specialty coffee, this is a real cost.

The Encore and its variants retain a modest amount by design , acceptable for most home use, but worth noting if you grind multiple coffees in sequence or dose by weight with precision. If you're doing single-dose work with expensive beans, the retention question is worth investigating before you commit. The broader [coffee grinder landscape](/grinders/) includes lower-retention options at higher price points.

### Build Quality and Longevity

Baratza's reputation in the home grinder market rests partly on their parts availability and repairability. Unlike most kitchen appliance manufacturers, they sell replacement burrs, hoppers, and motors directly , a grinder that can be serviced is a grinder that lasts ten years rather than two.

This matters when comparing Baratza to brands entering from adjacent categories, where build quality and long-term support are less established. A grinder is not a consumable. The cost per use over five years looks very different from the sticker price, and a repairable machine always wins that calculation.

## Buying Guide

### Filter or Espresso , or Both

This is the decision that branches everything else. Filter brewing , drip, pour-over, French press, AeroPress , is forgiving in the grind range. The steps between settings can be relatively coarse and still produce good results because extraction in these methods is slower and the recipe tolerates some variation. Espresso is the opposite: a small change in grind size shifts extraction dramatically, and the grinder must offer fine enough increments to track those changes intentionally.

The standard Encore was designed for filter. The ESP and ESP Pro were designed with espresso in the picture. Buying a filter-optimized grinder for espresso means you will hit its limits quickly. Buying an espresso-capable grinder for filter only is spending more than necessary. Be specific about your primary use before you choose.

### How Many Brews Per Day

Grinder capacity , both hopper size and motor duty cycle , is worth considering relative to how much coffee you actually make. A single-cup household grinding once in the morning will never stress any of these machines. A household making four to six cups per morning, or one that grinds for guests regularly, will notice the smaller hopper capacity on compact models and will occasionally run the motor longer than ideal.

None of the Encore variants are designed for high-volume use, and none of them will survive being treated as commercial equipment. For home use, they are entirely adequate. If you are grinding for more than three or four people daily, the Virtuoso+ handles the load more comfortably and the motor is better matched to regular extended use.

### Grind Settings: Stepped vs. Stepless

The Encore lineup uses stepped adjustment , discrete numbered positions rather than a continuous dial. That is a feature, not a limitation. Stepped adjustment means you can record your setting, return to it reliably, and communicate it to someone else. When you find the grind that works for your pour-over, you know it's setting 18 and you can go back to it every morning without guesswork.

Stepless grinders offer finer resolution but require more attention and note-taking to reproduce results. For most home users, stepped is more practical. The ESP Pro adds a micro-adjust ring that effectively adds resolution within each macro step, giving you the repeatability of stepped adjustment with additional fine-tuning capability.

### Single-Dose Work and Retention

High-retention grinders waste your best beans. If you're buying specialty coffee at premium prices, a grinder that holds back a meaningful dose inside the burr chamber and mixes it with the next grind is effectively diluting what you paid for. For single-origin exploration or households that switch between multiple coffees, retention becomes a real consideration.

The Encore and its variants have modest retention , acceptable for most home use, but not negligible. For serious single-dose work, there are lower-retention options at higher price points, and the [full range of coffee grinders](/grinders/) worth considering extends well beyond the Encore line. If retention is your primary concern, that's worth investigating separately.

### Maintenance Expectations

Burr grinders require periodic cleaning. Coffee oils coat the burrs and the grinding chamber over time, affecting grind quality and eventually taste. The Encore is easier to clean than most , the hopper removes without tools and the upper burr carrier lifts out for access. A brush and occasional deeper clean every few weeks is realistic for daily use.

Baratza's parts availability is a genuine advantage here. Replacement burrs, hoppers, and grind chambers are sold directly, which means a grinder that develops a fault is repairable rather than disposable. That support structure is one reason the Encore has held its position in this category for so long , you can maintain it, and Baratza makes it easy to do so.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between the Baratza Encore and the Encore ESP?

The standard [Baratza Encore](product:baratza-encore-coffee-grinder-zcg485blk) is calibrated for filter brewing , drip, pour-over, French press , with 40 stepped settings covering coarse to medium-fine. The [Encore ESP](product:baratza-encore-esp-coffee-grinder) shifts that calibration toward espresso: the fine end goes finer, the adjustment steps are smaller in the espresso range, and the coarse end is slightly compressed. If your primary method is espresso or a mix of espresso and filter, the ESP is the better fit. If you only brew filter, the standard Encore is sufficient and costs less.
### Is the Baratza Encore good enough for espresso?
The standard Encore can grind fine enough to pull an espresso shot, but the step size in the fine range is large enough that dialing in precisely is difficult. Most users find they land between settings rather than on one. The Encore ESP and ESP Pro address this with a finer range and smaller increments. For casual espresso , milk drinks where small extraction differences are masked , the standard Encore is workable.
### What is the difference between the Encore ESP and the Encore ESP Pro?

Both grinders cover the espresso range, but the [ESP Pro](product:baratza-encore-esp-pro-coffee) adds a micro-adjustment ring on top of the standard macro adjustment dial. That second ring allows fine-tuning within each numbered step rather than jumping between them. In practice, it gives you meaningfully more resolution for dialing in espresso without losing the repeatability of stepped adjustment. The standard [ESP](product:baratza-encore-esp-coffee-grinder) is capable for most home espresso users; the ESP Pro is for those who want to work more precisely.
### How does the Baratza Virtuoso+ compare to the Encore lineup?

The [Virtuoso+](product:baratza-virtuoso-coffee-grinder-zcg587blk) uses a larger, higher-tolerance burr set than any Encore variant, which produces more consistent particle distribution , the practical result is cleaner, more repeatable cups, especially for filter brewing. It also includes a digital timer for automated dose grinding. The Virtuoso+ is not optimized for espresso in the way the ESP models are, so it is best understood as the premium filter grinder in the Baratza home lineup rather than a direct Encore upgrade for all use cases.
### Should I choose a burr grinder or a blade grinder for coffee?

For coffee quality, a burr grinder is not a premium option , it is the correct tool. Blade grinders produce an inconsistent mix of particle sizes that makes controlled extraction impossible; the result is coffee that is simultaneously over- and under-extracted regardless of recipe or technique. A blade grinder like the [KitchenAid combo pack](product:kitchenaid-blade-coffee-grinder-and) is genuinely useful for spices, but it is not a substitute for a burr grinder for coffee. Even the most affordable burr grinder produces categorically better results than any blade model.
